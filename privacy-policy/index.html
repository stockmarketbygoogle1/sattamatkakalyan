Privacy Policy

This page explains how SattaMatkaKalyan.online handles information related to visitors and website usage.

Last updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

SattaMatkaKalyan.online is an independent informational website focused on Nagaland Lottery result information, historical archives, calendars and verification guidance. We respect the privacy of visitors and aim to keep the collection and use of information transparent and accurate.

By using this website, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy. This policy is intended to match the site's actual implementation and does not invent services that are not present on the website.

2. Information We Collect

Visitors do not need to create an account to browse the publicly available content.

Like most websites, standard server or hosting infrastructure may automatically process basic technical information when you visit a page, such as:

  • IP address or approximate network location
  • Browser and device information
  • Pages requested and referring pages
  • Date and time of requests
  • Basic technical and performance information

This website does not currently require users to submit personal contact forms or create login accounts through the published pages.

3. Cookies

The published pages of SattaMatkaKalyan.online are primarily static informational pages. Any cookies set by the hosting environment or your browser are limited to ordinary website operation.

This Privacy Policy does not claim that third-party advertising or analytics cookies are installed unless such tools are actually present on the website. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

4. Analytics

At the time of this policy update, the published site files do not include third-party analytics tags such as Google Analytics or similar measurement scripts. If analytics tools are added in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated to describe them.

5. External Links

Our website may contain links to external websites, including official sources and other third-party websites. Once you leave SattaMatkaKalyan.online, the privacy practices of the destination website apply. We are not responsible for the privacy policies or practices of external websites.

6. Third-Party Services

The website is hosted on third-party infrastructure. Hosting providers may process standard server logs and technical data according to their own policies.

This Privacy Policy does not claim that advertising networks, tracking pixels or marketing platforms are installed on the site unless those services are actually present. If such services are added later, this page will be updated.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to maintain the security and integrity of the website. However, no internet transmission or online storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Children's Privacy

This website is not intentionally designed to collect personal information from children. We do not knowingly request unnecessary personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when website functionality, services or applicable requirements change. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised update date.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or privacy practices on the website, please use our contact page.
