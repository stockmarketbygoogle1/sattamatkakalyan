Corrections & Feedback

If you notice an incorrect, outdated or incomplete piece of information, you can note the relevant page and explain the issue when a contact method is available.

Please include the page URL and enough information for the issue to be reviewed.

What to Include in a Message

To help us understand your request, include:

  • The page or section you are referring to.
  • A short explanation of the issue or feedback.
  • The relevant date or result period, if applicable.
  • A reliable source or reference when reporting a factual correction.
Important: For official lottery information, result confirmation and authoritative publications, please consult the official Nagaland State Lotteries source.
